Where does this exchange rate come from?

EuroCalc uses the European Central Bank (ECB) reference rate — the official mid-market rate published daily. This is the "real" exchange rate, without bank markups or hidden fees.

Why is the rate different at my bank?

Banks and payment services add a spread (markup) on top of the ECB rate — typically 1.5%–4%. That's how they make profit on currency exchange. Services like Wise and Revolut charge much smaller fees (0.3%–0.5%), making them significantly cheaper for international transfers.

How often is the rate updated?

The ECB publishes rates every business day at approximately 16:00 CET. Our calculator fetches the latest rate automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about EUR exchange rates
What is the EUR/CZK rate today?
The current EUR/CZK rate is shown live at the top of this page. It updates daily based on ECB data.
Why is PayPal's rate worse?
PayPal typically adds a 3–4% currency conversion fee on top of a less favourable base rate. On a €1,000 transfer, that's €30–40 lost to fees.
What is the best service for EUR exchange?
For most people in Europe, Wise (formerly TransferWise) or Revolut offer the lowest fees — typically 0.3%–0.5%. For cash exchange, compare local exchange offices using the ECB rate as a benchmark.
Is the ECB rate the same as the market rate?
Yes — the ECB rate is calculated from actual market transactions and represents the fair mid-market rate. No retail customer can exchange at exactly this rate, but it's the best reference point.
Can I use this calculator for business invoicing?
Yes. The ECB rate is widely accepted for VAT calculations and cross-border EU invoicing. For official tax purposes, always check your local tax authority's published rate.
What currencies does EuroCalc support?
We cover all major EUR pairs: CZK, PLN, HUF, USD, GBP, CHF, RON and more. All rates come from the ECB.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our calculators
Are these calculators free?
Yes, all calculators on EuroCalc are completely free. No registration or signup required.
How accurate are the results?
Results use standard financial formulas. For mortgage calculations, actual bank offers may vary based on your credit score, insurance, and specific bank policies.
Which EU VAT rate should I use?
Use the rate of the country where the transaction takes place. Slovakia: 20%, Czech Republic: 21%, Poland: 23%, Germany: 19%, France & Austria: 20%.
What is annuity vs differentiated payment?
Annuity: equal payments every month (easier to budget). Differentiated: larger payments at start that decrease over time (less total interest paid).
